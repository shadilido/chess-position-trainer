3. Fair play and behaviour

When playing online you agree not to:

  • use a chess engine, another person, or any other outside help during a live game;

  • use offensive, hateful, sexual or impersonating display names;
  • harass other players, deliberately let your clock run out to waste their time, or disrupt games;
  • attack, overload, reverse-engineer or try to get unauthorised access to the service or its database.

We may reset ratings, rename, suspend or delete accounts that break these rules, without notice.

How fair play is checked

  • While a live game is running, the app locks its own bot, reviews and analysis (also in other tabs of the same browser).
  • The app records how often each player leaves the game screen during their own turn. Your opponent sees this after the game.
  • After each game, your opponent’s app compares your moves with a chess engine (how often you played its first choice on non-obvious moves, and how much your moves cost on average) and stores the result. Accounts flagged by several different players are marked for opponents and reviewed.
  • Results and ratings are checked by the server and by both players’ apps. A game with an illegal move or a faked result does not count and is reported automatically.
  • You can report an opponent from the result screen. Reports are reviewed by the operator; automated checks alone never lead to a suspension.

4. Games and ratings

Ratings are an estimate for matchmaking and fun. They are not official chess ratings. Games can end because of connection problems (for example, if a player disconnects or runs out of time). We don’t change results after a game has finished, except where cheating is found.
