Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Short version: Training against the bot happens entirely on your device, and nothing about it is sent anywhere. Data is only stored online if you use online play (sign-in, matchmaking, friend lobbies): your account, display name, rating and online games. There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data. You can delete your account at any time inside the app.

1. Who is responsible

Position Trainer (“the app”) is a free, non-commercial hobby project published by its developer on GitHub (github.com/shadilido/chess-position-trainer), referred to here as “we”. For any privacy question or request, open an issue on that GitHub page, or use the contact details given there.

2. What happens on your device only

The chess engine (Stockfish), the training positions, your training statistics, game history, settings and any background photo you choose are stored only in your browser’s local storage on your device. They are never uploaded. Your background photo never leaves your device. You can remove this data by using “Reset statistics & history” in Settings or by clearing the website data in your browser.

4. Legal basis (EU / EEA users)

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): providing your account and online games, which you request.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): keeping the service secure and preventing abuse.

5. Service providers

Online play uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC, acting as our processor. Data may be processed on servers outside the EU, including in the USA. Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which receives your IP address when you load the site; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use no advertising or analytics cookies. The app uses your browser’s local storage and IndexedDB only for things that are strictly necessary: your settings, training data, offline files and, if you sign in, your login session (stored by Firebase Authentication).

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Matchmaking and lobby entries: removed when a match starts or is cancelled (stale entries are cleared automatically).
  • Online game records: kept so both players can see results; they may remain after you delete your account, showing only the display name you used then. Ask us via GitHub if you want them removed.

8. Your rights

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Account → Delete account), which removes your profile and sign-in. You can also change your display name there. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data and to data portability, and the right to complain to a data protection authority. To exercise these rights, contact us via GitHub as described in section 1.

9. Children

Online play is not intended for children under 16 without the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has created an account without consent, contact us and we will delete it. Offline training doesn’t require an account and stores nothing online.

10. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Database access is restricted by security rules so that players can only change their own profile and the games they play in. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be mentioned in the app or on the GitHub page.
